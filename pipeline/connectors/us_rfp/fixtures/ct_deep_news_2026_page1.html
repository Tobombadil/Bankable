  • 10/21/2026 Connecticut Celebrates International Bat Week October 24 - 31

    (HARTFORD)—The Connecticut Department of Energy and Environmental Protection (DEEP) is celebrating the importance of bats during Bat Week, an international event held annually during the week before Halloween. Bats face serious threats, including catastrophic population declines across North America driven largely by white-nose syndrome. These often-misunderstood animals play a critical role in maintaining healthy ecosystems and provide tremendous benefits to people, including natural pest control. Bat Week is an opportunity to highlight the importance of protecting bats and the simple actions everyone can take to help protect these important species.

  • 10/14/2026 DEEP Announces Increased Incentive Levels For CHEAPR Program Effective October 15th

    (HARTFORD) — The Connecticut Department of Energy and Environmental Protection (DEEP) announced today that the standard incentive level for the popular Connecticut Hydrogen and Electric Vehicle Purchase Rebate (CHEAPR) program will be changing effective October 15, 2026. The CHEAPR program offers incentives to Connecticut residents who purchase or lease an eligible new or used battery electric (BEV) or plug-in hybrid electric (PHEV) vehicle from a licensed Connecticut automobile dealership or original equipment manufacturer.

  • 10/6/2026 DEEP and CT State Library Celebrate Digital Inclusion Week, Announce Laptops Becoming Available for Loan Through Laptop ConneCT Program

    (HARTFORD)—The Connecticut Department of Energy and Environmental Protection (DEEP) joined the Connecticut State Library today in celebrating Digital Inclusion Week, an annual nationwide event dedicated to raising awareness about digital access and the importance of inclusion in our increasingly digital world. At the event, DEEP and the CT State Library highlighted the State’s efforts to close the digital divide, including the recently announced Laptop ConneCT program, through which laptops will be available for short- or long-term loans to qualifying individuals at participating public libraries around the state. Laptops are already available at some participating libraries, with availability expected at most locations by the end of October and at all participating locations by the end of the year.

Rockport Natural Gas Project RFP

American Electric Power Service Corporation, as agent for Indiana Michigan Power Company, is soliciting proposals from bidders to construct, operate and maintain a natural gas pipeline lateral and associated natural gas facilities to the Rockport Natural Gas Project located in Rockport, Indiana. These Gas Facilities will be utilized to supply the Plant with natural gas as the primary fuel source. The Company is also seeking offers of mainline firm transportation capacity.

2024 RFPs

I&M has issued Requests for Proposals ("2024 RFPs") which seeks to secure up to 4,000 MW of generation resources by the 2029/30 capacity planning year to support I&M's capacity and energy needs in response to new load growth. Under the RFPs, I&M seeks a diverse portfolio of resources to support the timing and energy and capacity characteristics associated with near term load expectations.

## 2. The rule applied to silent state sites

Eight sources (Georgia, Louisiana's DCE page, North Dakota DMR Class VI, Wyoming, Oklahoma, New Mexico, North
Dakota PSC, Iowa) publish no reuse terms at all. Each was read in full: the agency page, its footer and the state
portal's policy, disclaimer or privacy page. What they carry is a warranty disclaimer, a copyright line and, in
Wyoming's case, "All Rights Reserved".

The register has a precedent. `docs/13` §1.5 read NYISO's notice, which "does not confer any license" and
"expressly reserves" rights but prohibits nothing for data, as "permissive by omission" and classed it
`attribution-restricted`, derived-only. These state sites say less than NYISO does. I propose the same class and
rule for all eight:

- **Class** `attribution-restricted`; manifest `reuse: attribution`, `publication: derived_only` (or `none` where
  robots closes the route, as for Iowa's EFS).
- **Publish** derived facts only (names, operator organisations, county, status, dates, volumes), credited to the
  agency and linked to its page; never a mirror of the agency's PDFs, tables or images; organisation names only,
  never natural persons (`docs/13` §5).
- **Hold before paid use**, as for Wisconsin and Ohio parcels (`docs/06` §2.3, §2.5): before
  `PLATFORM_POSTURE=commercial` or a Pro sale that includes these rows, counsel confirms the reading (item 1 in §5),
  or the agency confirms in writing. `docs/26` §5's switch-back check should list these rows.

*Why this is defensible, and its limit (moderate confidence).* With no terms there is no contract to breach
(`docs/13` §3.2: browsewrap needs terms and notice), and the facts in a permit list are not copyrightable
(*Feist*, `docs/13` §1.4). State works can carry copyright (17 U.S.C. §105 does not reach states), which is why we
take facts and never the documents. The limit is commercial use: silence is weaker evidence for a paid product than
for a free one, hence the hold.

The owner can reject this rule; then all eight stay `unknown` and become eight agency emails. I recommend the rule:
it is how NYISO is already handled, and the emails would ask agencies to grant what most of them never claimed to
withhold.

   and a copyright line, in Wyoming "All Rights Reserved") permits derived facts on a free public site, and whether
   that holds for a paid product. Covers Georgia, Louisiana DCE, North Dakota DMR and PSC, Wyoming, Oklahoma, New
   Mexico and Iowa. Same question as `docs/13` §1.5 for NYISO.
2. **TCEQ's "as long as a fee is not charged to access our material"** (§1.1): whether it reaches a paid tier that
   shows facts derived from TCEQ public-domain content.
3. **North Dakota's subscription agreement** (§1.4): whether well facts obtained from a third party (EPA, FracFocus,
   a researcher) are free of the "substantially duplicate OGD subscription services" clause. Not urgent: the
   proposal is not to use the paid data.
4. **ODbL share-alike for ANEEL** (§1.8): whether fusing ANEEL auction or RALIE rows into Inframation's record
   store makes a "Derivative Database" that must be offered under ODbL, or a "Collective Database" if the rows stay
   separate records. Same question as OpenStreetMap (`docs/66` row 13).
5. **NSTA's "non-commercially"** (§1.7): whether Inframation's posture is non-commercial in the User Agreement's
   sense. Same question as `docs/13` §7 item 15.
6. **Bans on linking** (SECI, IPP Office, §1.8): enforceability of a term forbidding hyperlinks. We comply by not
   linking; counsel only if the owner wants these sources without asking.
7. **ENTSO-E** (§1.8): whether the www.entsoe.eu terms govern `tyndp2026.entsoe.eu`, and whether TEN-E publication
   duties make the project list reusable.
8. **SPP and ISO-NE**, already `docs/13` §7 items 6 and 15: whether Inframation's free site is a "commercial
   publication" (SPP) or a "non-personal use" (ISO-NE). The letters in `docs/37` §5 ask the operators instead.
9. **Kansas K.S.A. 45-230** (§1.4): its reach to operator names taken from KGS data, if those names were ever used
   for outreach. Statute not read first-hand here.

## 6. Assumptions

- A1. A state portal's footer pages are the complete terms for an agency site that links no terms of its own
  (Georgia, North Dakota, Iowa, Oklahoma). If an agency posts terms later, the row is re-read.
- A2. The ArcGIS item's "NSTA Open User Licence" is the NSTA User Agreement (June 2023 PDF): the linked page names
  no other licence.
- A3. The ENTSO-E site terms govern its TYNDP subdomain, whose served HTML carries none of its own.
- A4. Search results and third-party copies used for Our Grid Future, the IEA and Arizona are secondary and are
  not treated as terms; each is marked where used.
- A5. The register's robots readings of 2026-09 (SONRIS, EFS, `pipeline.wyo.gov`) still hold; they were not
  re-requested.
- A6. ANEEL's CKAN `license_title` is ANEEL's licence statement for the dataset, as data.gov's `license` field is
  for US federal datasets.

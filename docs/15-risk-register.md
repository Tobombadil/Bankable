## 0. How to read this register

1. **Every risk cites where it comes from in the repo** — a decisions-log row (by date), a section, a known-gap
   item or a measured number. A risk with no citation is an opinion and does not belong here.
2. **Scores are explained, never bare.** Likelihood and impact are each `H`/`M`/`L` (3/2/1) with a one-line
   reason. **Exposure** is the product (1–9). Likelihood is *over the next 90 days, on the current state of the
   repo*; impact is *if it happens*, not discounted by the mitigation already in place — the mitigation column
   is where that credit is taken.
3. **Leading indicator** is something a machine or a person can observe *before* the loss: a query, a count, a
   file, a date passing. If a risk has no observable indicator today, the indicator column says so and the
   planned column says what would create one.
4. **Mitigation in place today** cites the module, test or document that enforces it. "Planned" is what is
   named somewhere in the repo as the next step; it is not a promise of delivery.
5. **Owner** is who acts, not who worries: `owner` (the founder; anything needing a signature, an account, a
   payment or a legal reading), `coordinator`, or a named agent role from `docs/03` §2.
6. **Review date** defaults to the monthly operations review (`docs/63` §7.3), first one **2026-10-26**, and is
   earlier where a dated trigger exists.
7. Categories: `legal/data`, `technical`, `commercial`, `operational`, `security/privacy`. Ids are stable;
   a retired risk keeps its id and is struck through, never renumbered.

Ranked by exposure, ties broken by how little the mitigation in place covers.

1. **R-05 No counsel engaged, and the platform now proceeds on the owner's own legal reading** (legal/data,
   9). Fifteen counsel items in `docs/13` §7 plus eleven in `docs/13-legal-outreach-and-social.md` §11 have

   change-event alerts (item 6). `docs/13` §7 item 7 is the sharp edge: the register itself documents actual
   knowledge of every restriction, so a wrong reading is a breach with knowledge, not an oversight. Nothing is
   public yet, which is the only reason the likelihood of *loss* is not already realised.
2. **R-17 Single-founder key-person** (commercial, 9). Every account, secret, signature, payment, counsel
   engagement and outbound message runs through one person (`docs/10` A-2; `docs/40` §2 lists about 45
   owner-only done-checks, all blank since 2026-09-13; the owner was away for a week from 2026-09-19 and the
   coordinator was given merge authority to compensate). There is no second key-holder for the age keys,
   the registrar, Stripe or Attio because none of those exist yet — which is also the moment to design it in.
3. **R-11 Nothing is deployed and no operational procedure has been executed** (technical, 6). No cloud
   account, no managed Postgres, no migration run against real Postgres, no image built, no deploy, rollback,
   restore or secret rotation ever executed (`docs/60` §10 "Last executed: not yet" on all five runbooks; §11
   items 1–9). Every latency and throughput number in the decisions log is a SQLite sandbox measurement
   (`docs/00-PLAN.md` 2026-09-15: "Postgres figures come with the first deploy"). Impact is schedule and
   surprise rather than data loss, which keeps it off the top.
4. **R-18 The commercial thesis is unmeasured and there is no revenue path while the posture is
   noncommercial** (commercial, 6). `docs/11` §3's 1–2 % free-to-Pro estimate rests on a delay mechanism that
   no longer exists (`docs/00-PLAN.md` 2026-09-20: "unsupported"); M-4 and M-5 (`docs/10` §5) have no numbers;
   paid tiers are inactive by design under `noncommercial` (`docs/26` §3(i)); "a new commercial function later"
   (owner, 2026-09-21) is undefined. The replacement signal the owner set — 2 % of company/asset page views
   converting to registrations (2026-09-18 decision 8) — cannot be read until something is deployed.
5. **R-06 A licence-gate breach (M-11 > 0)** (legal/data, 6). Likelihood is only medium because the audit of
   2026-09-18 found two live leak paths (events feed and Pro digest emails bypassing the record-level gate,
   `docs/50` §3.1) in code that had passed review, and new read surfaces keep arriving (assets, lines, the
   ownership walk, coverage). Impact is high: it is the S1 definition in `docs/04` S-9 and a `CLAUDE.md`
   guardrail. The nightly audit that would make this observable is **in build 2026-09-26**, not done; `docs/40`
   §4 row 11 still reads "not located".

Source breakage (R-12) scores the same 6 and is the one most likely to *happen* first — several breakages
were observed in the first two weeks — but the product's own design (last-good data keeps serving, `docs/20`
§12) caps its impact at missed change events, so it sits sixth.

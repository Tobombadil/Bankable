Privacy notice

What personal data this platform stores, the lawful basis for each use, how long it is kept, and how to have it deleted.

Infraqueue is a placeholder codename, not the permanent product name. Last updated {{ last_updated }}.

{{ m.section_heading("1.", "What we store") }}
Accounts
Email address, name, and a password hash (never the password itself). Used to sign you in and to identify which saved searches and alerts belong to you.
Alerts
Your saved-search definitions (the filters you asked to be notified about) and a delivery log of alerts sent -- recipient, saved search, the matched records, and send status. Every alert email carries a one-click unsubscribe link tied to that saved search.
Intake and reports
If you submit a project or a report through the site, we store the contact block you provide (name and email) alongside the submission so we can follow up on it.
CRM (business contacts)
Where our own outreach is concerned, we hold business contact details (name, work email, job title) sourced from public professional sources such as filings and company sites -- never a personal email or a private social profile. This is the CRM system of record's People object; see the data-handling rules in docs/34-crm-system-of-record.md §2.2. The platform is not the system of record for this data; the CRM is.

We do not store more personal data than the categories above. We do not sell personal data.

{{ m.section_heading("2.", "Why we use it") }}
  • Contract / providing the service -- your account and alert data: we need it to run the account you asked for and deliver the alerts you configured.
  • Consent -- you choose which saved searches send email alerts, and can withdraw that consent at any time via the unsubscribe link on every alert email or the form below.
  • Legitimate interest -- business contact details reached through public professional sources, used only to reach out about a specific, relevant project (never for unrelated marketing), per the outreach rules in docs/13-legal-outreach-and-social.md §7.2. A recorded consent basis (legitimate interest, consent, or existing customer) is kept per contact in the CRM.

Every outbound message we send states who we are, how we found you, what we want, and how to stop hearing from us -- the five points in docs/13 §7.2.

{{ m.section_heading("3.", "Retention and deletion") }}

We keep account and alert data for as long as your account is active. A deletion request -- from you, or from someone named in a filing we index -- is handled as a deletion task: it removes or redacts the personal-data fields above from every surface and export, and the action itself is recorded (US-910). Sessions and API keys are revoked and alerts are suppressed as part of the same task.

To request deletion or ask a question about your data, email {{ privacy_email }}. To stop email alerts for a specific saved search without deleting your account, use the unsubscribe link in that alert's email, or the unsubscribe page.

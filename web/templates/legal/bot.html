Our data reader

Inframation lists energy projects, RFPs and infrastructure from public registers. A small program reads those registers on a schedule. This page is where its User-Agent points, so a site owner can see who we are and how to reach us.

{{ m.section_heading("1.", "How it identifies itself") }}

Every request carries this User-Agent, with the product token Inframation:

{{ user_agent }}

{{ m.section_heading("2.", "The rules it follows") }}
  • It reads only sources whose terms we have read and recorded. The list, with each source's licence, is on About & sources.
  • On websites it follows robots.txt. Rules for Inframation come first, then the * group.
  • It sends about one request per second to a host, usually far fewer: most sources are read once a day, week or month.
  • It never signs in, never registers on a bid or procurement portal, and never solves or works around a CAPTCHA or other challenge. A refused or challenged request stops that run.
  • It shows facts with a link back and the source's credit line, never a copy of your documents.
{{ m.section_heading("3.", "Asking it to stop") }}

Add this to your robots.txt and the next run will respect it:

User-agent: Inframation
Disallow: /

Or write to {{ contact_email }}, and we will stop reading your site and remove what we hold from it within five business days. Other ways to reach us.

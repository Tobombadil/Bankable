Grid interconnection points

Where proposed projects connect to the grid, as each interconnection queue names it: a substation bus or a tap on a line. A project connects at the point; it is not built there. Each row adds up the projects queued at that point.

Totals count only projects this page may show; a project withheld under its source licence, or not yet published, is not in any number here. Spellings of one point in one queue are grouped by a documented rule; points are never merged across queues. Methodology.

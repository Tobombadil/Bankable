Placement and defaults

How records are placed and shown
  • A record sits where its source puts it: an exact coordinate where the source gives one and its licence allows it to be shown, otherwise the centre of its county (US Census Gazetteer 2024, public domain). A record with neither a usable county nor a state is counted as Unplaced, never guessed.
  • The map and the Projects list open on active projects, announced to under construction. Withdrawn and cancelled ones are one switch away: most of a queue’s history is withdrawn requests, and a default view of them would map what died rather than what is active.
  • NESO’s TEC Register gives no US-style county or state, so its rows count as Unplaced rather than guessed.
  • Clusters on the map are computed by the server; the page draws what the API returns.
  • Basemap tiles come from OpenStreetMap. World-country and US-state outlines (Natural Earth and US Census Bureau TIGERweb, both public domain) are drawn beneath them, so the map stays legible if the tiles cannot load.

Channel switches

Owner only (US-802 AC2). Default is off for every channel: nothing publishes without a human approval unless an owner has switched it on here, for named event types, each of which must have passed graduation (200 reviewed posts over 30 days, edit rate at most 5%, no wrong-fact rejection; docs/32 §4.6). The worker re-checks graduation for every post. LinkedIn never auto-publishes. An auto-published post carries the line “Auto-generated summary from [source]; not human-reviewed.”

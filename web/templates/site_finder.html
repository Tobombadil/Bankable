How the bands are found

Every OpenStreetMap power line of 69 kV and up is cut into half-mile segments. A segment passes when its line meets the voltage floor, a substation lies within the distance chosen, and, with the floodplain left out, FEMA’s digital flood map covers its band and at most 90% of the band is in the 1% annual-chance floodplain. Neighbouring passing segments of one line join into one band; the floodplain is cut out of the band drawn on the map.

In Texas, appraisal-district parcels (compiled by TxGIO) show who holds the land beside the line without naming anyone: a holding is one owner’s parcels that touch or lie within 30 m of each other. “Contiguous acres” keeps the segments whose band touches that much land, from one holding or from neighbouring holdings up to the owners chosen. {{ owner_note }}

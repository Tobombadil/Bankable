About Inframation

Energy market intelligence from public registers.

How it works

One record per project, every fact sourced. Each register is read on its own schedule; rows from different registers that describe one real-world project are fused into one record, and every fact keeps its source, the date we read it and its licence. Registers whose terms do not allow republishing are linked to, never copied.

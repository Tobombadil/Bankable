Method notes for this prototype

  • Placement on the map follows source precision: an exact coordinate where the source supplies one and its licence allows raw publication; otherwise a county centroid (US Census Gazetteer 2024, public domain); otherwise a state-level aggregate marker; records with neither a usable county nor a state are listed under “Unplaced”.
  • Clustering on the map is computed client-side by MapLibre's built-in clustering rather than the server-side /v1/proposals/geo endpoint described in docs/23, since this prototype has no database or API service yet.
  • NESO TEC Register rows have no US-style county or state field in the source and are listed under Unplaced (GB) rather than guessed at.
  • The map's basemap tiles come from OpenStreetMap; a lightweight world-country and US-state outline layer (Natural Earth and US Census Bureau TIGERweb, both public domain) is drawn underneath them so the map stays legible if the tile provider is ever unreachable.
